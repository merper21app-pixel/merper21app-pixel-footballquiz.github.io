Privacy Policy

Last updated · 26 July 2026

This policy explains how the Football Quiz app handles information. By playing, you agree to it.

1 No account needed

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email, phone number, or any other personal identifying information on our servers. The App has no servers of its own.

2 Your progress stays on your phone

Your streak, XP, level, rank, achievements, statistics, chosen avatar, and settings are stored locally on your device only. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the App removes this data.

3 Advertising

The App is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may use information such as a device advertising identifier, general location (from IP address), and app usage data, in line with Google's policies.

Where required (such as the EEA and the UK), the App shows a consent form before serving personalised ads, so you can accept or decline. Google's practices: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4 Analytics

The App may use Google's services to understand aggregate, anonymous usage. This does not identify you personally.

5 Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6 Your choices

You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings, under Google → Ads. Uninstalling the App clears all locally stored data.

7 Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top reflects the latest revision.

8 Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at merper21app@gmail.com
