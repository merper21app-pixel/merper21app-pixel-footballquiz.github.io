Privacy Policy — Football Quiz

Last updated: 26 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Football Quiz mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. No account, no personal data collected by us

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifying information on our servers. In fact, the App has no servers of its own.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress — including your streak, experience points, level, rank, achievements, statistics, chosen avatar, and settings — is stored locally on your device only. This information stays on your phone and is not transmitted to us. If you uninstall the App, this data is removed.

3. Advertising

The App is free and supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and use certain information, such as a device advertising identifier, general location (derived from IP address), and app usage data, in accordance with Google's policies.

In regions where it is required (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the App presents a consent form before serving personalised ads, allowing you to accept or decline. You can review Google's practices here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Analytics

The App may use Google's services to understand aggregate, anonymous usage (for example, how many players complete a quiz). This information does not identify you personally.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices

You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalisation through your device settings, under Google → Ads. You can clear all locally stored data by uninstalling the App.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.
